nomadicgamer

nomadicgamer posts short highlight clips from live GTA 6 streams to YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels.

Clips are found automatically by a personal tool (nomadicgamer clipper) that watches public live streams, picks the moments where chat and audio spike, and formats them as vertical videos with captions. The tool only posts to nomadicgamer's own accounts.

Watch: YouTube @nomadicgamerclips

Streamers who want a clip removed can open an issue at github.com/xsephtion/nomadicgamer/issues and it will be taken down.
