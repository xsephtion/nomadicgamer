Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

nomadicgamer clipper ("the app") is a personal publishing tool. Its only user is its operator.

What the app accesses

When the operator connects their own YouTube, TikTok or Instagram account, the app receives an access token that lets it upload videos and read basic profile information (such as the account name) for that account. Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer and are never shared or sold.

Viewers and streamers

The app reads public live-stream chat in aggregate (message counts and reactions) to detect highlights. It does not store chat usernames or build profiles of anyone.

Data sharing

No personal data is sold or shared with third parties. Videos are sent only to the platform they are being posted on.

Deletion

The operator can revoke the app's access at any time from each platform's account settings, which invalidates the stored tokens.
